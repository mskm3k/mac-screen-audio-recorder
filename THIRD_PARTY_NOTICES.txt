Mac Screen Audio Recorder v1.0.2
Third-party / redistribution notes

1. This source archive does not bundle third-party libraries, packages, fonts,
   photographs, stock assets, or third-party application binaries.

2. The application uses Apple system frameworks at build/runtime:
   AppKit, ScreenCaptureKit, AVFoundation, CoreMedia, CoreVideo,
   CoreGraphics, and AudioToolbox.
   These frameworks are supplied by macOS/Xcode Command Line Tools and are not
   redistributed in this archive.

3. Resources/AppIcon-1024.png is the project-specific application icon and is
   distributed with this project subject to BRANDING.md in addition to LICENSE.txt.

4. No Apple certificate, provisioning profile, private key, Apple ID, email
   address, Development Team identifier, or authentication token is included.
